# **HR Workforce Planning & Attrition Analytics**

## **Overview**
This Power BI report was developed as part of the ZoomCharts HR Workforce Planning & Attrition Analytics Challenge.
The report provides an interactive view of workforce growth, employee attrition, workforce gaps, and recruitment activity. It helps HR and business leaders understand whether the organization is meeting its workforce plans, where staffing gaps exist, and which areas require closer attention.

## **Problem Statement**
Organizations need to maintain the right workforce capacity while managing employee turnover and recruitment demands.
This analysis addresses the challenge of understanding where actual workforce levels fall below plan, how employee exits are affecting workforce capacity, and whether recruitment activity is supporting workforce needs.

## **Business Questions Answered**
The report was designed to answer:

How does actual headcount compare with planned workforce levels?
How are hiring and termination activities changing over time?
What is the organization's attrition pattern?
Which tenure groups and roles experience higher attrition?
What are the main termination reasons?
How do voluntary and involuntary exits differ?
Which departments have the largest workforce gaps?
Where is recruitment demand highest?
How long does it take to fill open positions?

## **Tools & Methodology**

### *Tool Used*
* Power Query for data cleaning and Transformation
* Power BI for data modeling, analysis, and visualization
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for calculated measures and KPIs
* PowerPoint for report wireframe

The analysis used KPI cards, time-series analysis, departmental comparisons, tables, and interactive drillthroughs.
The report was structured into three areas: Workforce Overview → Attrition Analysis → Workforce Planning & Recruitment

This approach connects workforce movement with employee attrition and recruitment requirements to provide a broader view of workforce planning.

## **Key Insights**
Workforce levels remain below plan: The report shows a -20.63% headcount variance, indicating that actual workforce levels are below the relevant planned level.
Hiring activity is substantial: The organization recorded 1,200 hires and 206 terminations, but workforce gaps remain.
Workforce gaps vary by department: Finance has the largest percentage gap at -53.54%, followed by Data & Analytics at -48.04%.
Engineering has the highest recruitment demand: It has 506 open requisitions, the highest among the departments shown.
Mid-tenure employees show higher attrition: The 2–5 year tenure group has the highest displayed attrition rate.
Restructuring is the leading termination reason: Company restructuring accounts for the largest share of the recorded termination reasons.
Recruitment turnaround varies: Data & Analytics has the highest displayed average time to fill at 55.53 days, followed by Engineering at 53.89 days.
Attrition trend is declining: The displayed attrition trend moves downward across the reporting period, despite the overall attrition KPI of 33.12%.

## **Recommendations**

Monitor workforce gaps regularly and focus on departments significantly below planned headcount.
Investigate recruitment bottlenecks, particularly in departments with high open requisitions and longer time-to-fill.
Examine retention among 2–5 year employees to understand the factors contributing to higher attrition.
Separate restructuring-related exits from voluntary turnover when evaluating retention performance.
Use recruitment and workforce metrics together to monitor whether hiring activity is effectively closing staffing gaps.
Align workforce planning with salary budgets to understand the financial impact of staffing decisions.

## **Conclusion**

The analysis shows that workforce gaps, employee attrition, and recruitment activity are closely connected. While the organization is actively hiring, actual workforce levels remain below plan, with certain departments experiencing particularly large gaps and recruitment demands. The report provides HR leaders with a clearer view of where workforce and retention efforts require further investigation.
